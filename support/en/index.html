Common questions

My records are gone. Records are stored only on your device. They do not carry over if the app is deleted or you change devices. There is no account, so the developer has no copy.

How do I continue a round? Every tap is saved. Reopen the app and tap Continue on the home screen; it resumes from the first empty hole.

I tapped OB by mistake. Tap the ✕ on that hole's penalty chip to remove one penalty and one stroke.

What is the Peoria handicap? The app secretly picks hidden holes (12 of 18) and calculates a same-day handicap from them, so players of different skill can be ranked fairly. Turn it on when starting a round; the hidden holes are revealed on the results screen.

How are ties broken? The last nine holes are compared first, then hole by hole from the last hole backwards. In Peoria net rankings, the lower handicap wins a tie.

Nearby search cannot find my location. Allow location access for the app in your device settings. Without it, the app searches around the current map center.

Why are there ads? Ads keep the app free. Full-screen ads never appear while you are entering scores, only occasionally after you close a round's results.

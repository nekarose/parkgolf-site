Now, Park Golf — Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

한국어

Now, Park Golf ("the app") has no sign-up and no developer-run server. It does not ask for your name, phone number or email. Everything you record stays on your device. To show ads and maps, the companies listed below process some information. Here is exactly what goes where.

1. Stored only on your device

  • Your display name on scorecards (default "나")
  • Companion names you type in to tell players apart
  • Rounds — course name, date, strokes and penalties per hole, player names, local rules, Peoria hidden holes
  • Reservations — course, tee time, companion names, your notes
  • Courses you asked to be notified about
  • Settings — default double-par cap and Peoria handicap
  • A count of finished rounds used to space out ads, and the last app configuration file received

A nickname or surname is enough for companions. This data is deleted when you delete the app, and you can delete rounds, reservations and companions one by one inside the app. With no account or server, the developer has no copy, and records do not move to a new device.

2. Location

The app reads your current location only when you tap "Nearby courses" or "My location". It is used to measure distances to courses and to show your position on the map. It is not stored and not sent to the developer. Permission is requested the first time you tap; without it, the app searches around the map center instead. You can change the permission in your device settings at any time.

3. Maps

Maps are provided by NAVER Maps (NAVER Cloud Platform). While loading map tiles, NAVER may process device information and the map area being loaded, under NAVER's policies. The app does not send your location to NAVER separately; your position marker is drawn on the device.

  • NAVER Privacy Policy

4. Ads

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use device information such as the advertising identifier to select and show ads, under Google's policies.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Why we ask about tracking — on iOS, the app asks on first launch whether to allow tracking. If allowed, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used to choose personalized ads. If not, every feature still works; ads are simply not personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA and UK, an ad consent form is also shown on first launch.

5. App configuration file

On launch, the app reads a public configuration file on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) to check for new versions and ad spacing. None of your information is sent. As with any web request, GitHub may process standard access logs such as your IP address.

6. What we do not collect

  • Accounts, logins or passwords
  • Usage analytics — none are used
  • Contacts, photos or microphone
  • Payment information — there are no in-app purchases

7. Third parties

We do not sell or share your information. Only the following companies process what is needed to run the app, under their own policies.

On-device data stays until you delete it or the app. The developer keeps none of it.
